Why It Works For Your Clients

🛍️ Tax-free / VAT-refund shopping for non-EU visitors, including Indian passport holders

🚌 Free coach parking and group drop-off at every outlet

🗣️ Multilingual staff and, at flagship sites, dedicated group concierge desks

🎟️ Complimentary VIP discount cards typically available for group bookings

⏱️ A 2–3 hour stop fits naturally into a travel day, transfer day, or free afternoon

🏷️ Recognised international designer brands at outlet prices — a strong perceived-value add at no extra cost to your package

Good To Know

Most outlets keep standard retail hours 7 days a week, though a few (parts of Germany/Austria) restrict Sunday trading — worth confirming for weekend stops. Peak season weekends can mean coach queues, so an early-morning or weekday slot works best for larger groups.

Pitching This To Your Clients

  • Frame it as "free time with a purpose": instead of an unstructured afternoon, offer a shopping stop at a recognisable outlet on the way.
  • Bundle it with a photo-stop city your group is already visiting — Vienna, Venice, Rome, Milan, Salzburg and London all have an on-route outlet nearby.
  • Highlight the tax-free saving for Indian travellers — this is often the detail that tips a group toward saying yes.
  • Use it as a group-cohesion moment on MICE trips — an easy, low-effort shared activity between structured sessions.
  • Mention it in the sales pitch before departure, not just on the day — "an outlet shopping stop" is a line that helps close bookings.
